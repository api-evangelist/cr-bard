# C. R. Bard, Inc.

> C. R. Bard, Inc. was an American multinational developer, manufacturer and marketer of medical
> technologies across vascular, urology, oncology and surgical specialties. Founded in 1907 by
> Charles Russell Bard, it was acquired by Becton, Dickinson and Company (BD) on 29 December 2017
> for approximately $24 billion, ceased trading, and was delisted from the NYSE. It exists today
> only as a brand and product heritage inside BD's Interventional segment. **C. R. Bard publishes
> no API, no developer portal and no machine-readable contract of any kind, and has not since the
> acquisition.** This file is an API Evangelist profile of that absence, not a product index.
